The core identity & platform

NexusHub

The company's employee platform and sign-in provider: records, leave, requests, onboarding and news in one place, plus the identity every other app trusts.

hub.nexus-suite.internal · live showcase
Click to enlarge ⤢
Company feed with a "Please read" announcement, posts and likes Interactive Console

Employee records

Personal data is split into four privacy levels (public, employment, confidential and sensitive). Government IDs are encrypted and every confidential view is logged. Includes CSV import, an org chart and a directory.

Leave

Philippine statutory leave types, balances earned monthly or yearly, service-year steps, carry-over and conversion, a team calendar, iCal feeds and figures for payroll.

Request forms

A drag-and-drop form builder with versions and approval flows. Certificates are generated as PDFs with a letterhead, drawn signatures and a QR code anyone can verify.

Approvals engine

Step-by-step flows (manager, role or person), delegation while away, reminders after two working days and escalation after five. Other apps can use it too.

Onboarding & offboarding

Checklist templates on a timeline, a pre-hire portal for uploading requirements, probation reminders, clearance sign-offs and exit interviews.

Company feed

Posts, photos, mentions and likes. Announcements can be pinned and ask people to confirm they've read them. Includes moderation, birthdays and work anniversaries.

Roles from permissions

HR builds roles by choosing an access level for each module. Nobody can grant more access than they hold, and two-factor sign-in is required for every role.

Sign-in for every app

An OpenID Connect provider with PKCE and consent. Signing out in one app signs you out of the others (back-channel logout), and you can see and remove connected apps.

The connected hardware asset tracker

NexusInventory

IT asset inventory across sites: what you own, where it is, who holds it and what it's made of. Employees sign in through the Hub, and the employee list stays in sync with the Hub automatically.

inv.nexus-suite.internal · asset console
Click to enlarge ⤢
A server's specs, worked out from its parts Interactive Console

Assets & components

Laptops, servers, network gear, point-of-sale devices and parts. Each asset type has its own specs, and a server's specs are worked out from the parts installed in it.

Assign & acknowledge

Assign and check out equipment, then return it. The holder acknowledges what they received, and every change is kept in the asset's history.

QR labels & scanning

Print labels as PDF or for Zebra printers, scan them with a phone camera, and reissue a code if a label is lost. Large print batches run in the background.

Purchase orders

Draft, approve (by a different site admin), order, then receive straight into stock, with documents attached.

Sites & locations

Sites, floors, rooms and storage areas, with access limited to each person's site.

Offboarding clearance

When someone resigns, the Hub's checklist asks NexusInventory what they still hold, and the clearance task signs off by itself once everything is returned.

Precision delivery management

NexusProject

Projects, their teams and milestones, with progress that comes from the real work in NexusTask instead of someone's estimate. Status movement, an on-time or late timetable and delivery reports come next.

project.nexus-suite.internal · project view
Click to enlarge ⤢

Projects & teams

Each project has a code, status, priority, lead and dates. Members get a project role, and a project is visible only to its members and project admins.

Milestones

Delivery points in order, each with a due date and status. Tasks in NexusTask are filed under the open ones.

Progress from NexusTask

Tasks stay in NexusTask. NexusProject keeps a copy from NexusTask's task events, so the percentage done and the done, in progress and to do counts are always current.

Overview

Active projects, your projects, milestones at risk and overdue tasks on one page, with recent projects and their progress below.

Fluid collaborative productivity

NexusTask

Your tasks and your team's in one place: personal and team lists, boards, due dates and reminders. Tasks for a project are filed under it, and NexusProject sees their progress.

task.nexus-suite.internal · board view
Click to enlarge ⤢
A team list as a board Interactive Console

My tasks & Today

Tasks grouped by when they're due, a quick add, this week's done tasks, and a Today page with a progress ring where you tick tasks off.

Lists & boards

A personal list plus team lists you share. Drag tasks across the board's columns, or switch to a list with filters for status, person, priority and due date.

Company workflow

Admins set the statuses every task moves through, in board order. Each task gets a number (T-12) that you can search for.

Project tasks

File a task under a NexusProject project and milestone, and it's numbered by the project code (ENG-4). The project's team decides who can do what.

Calendar & saved views

A month and week calendar where you drag a task to another day. Search, then save a view so it stays in the sidebar.

Comments & files

Comments with @mentions, an activity history and attachments up to 10 MB. Files are stored outside the web root and shown only to people who can open the task.

Notifications

Hub notifications when you're assigned or mentioned or a task you follow changes, plus 08:00 reminders for tasks due tomorrow or overdue.

On the Hub home page

A My tasks card on NexusHub's home page asks NexusTask for your open tasks live, using a short-lived service token from the Hub.

System Architecture

One hub, any number of apps

Apps connect only to the Hub and never share secrets with each other. A shared client kit (a Symfony bundle) gives each new app all of this in an afternoon. NexusTask and NexusProject talk through it too: task events go out through the Hub, and service tokens let each read the other's API.

Quality gates on every commit

  • PHPStan level 8, with no baseline and no ignored errors
  • PHPUnit integration and web tests against real MySQL
  • PHP-CS-Fixer, linting and Doctrine schema checks
  • Hand-written, reversible database migrations
  • Contract tests: NexusProject checks itself against NexusTask's task event fixtures

Privacy & security

  • Privacy levels enforced in one place, with a log of every view of confidential data
  • Secrets encrypted at rest, with key rotation
  • Strict content security policy, CSRF protection, and login throttling and lockout
  • Employees can download their own data, and accept versioned privacy notices
  • Reviewed against OWASP ASVS level 2
